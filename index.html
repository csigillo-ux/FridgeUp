The story

One person, one fridge full of expired food

My name is Carmelo. FridgeUp! came out of a fridge full of forgotten, expired food — one more time throwing away food because I hadn't noticed in time. I'm not a programmer by trade: I learned Swift by writing this app, in my spare time, with help from AI. No team, no company: just me wanting to solve a problem of my own.
